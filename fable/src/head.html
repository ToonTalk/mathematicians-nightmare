The Mathematician’s Nightmare

The vision of Professor Squarepunt

A short film in three dimensions, after the story by Bertrand Russell in Nightmares of Eminent Persons (1954).

At any moment you can drag to watch from another side, and click a number to learn something about it.

The voices are computer-generated. The words retell Russell’s story; they are not his text.

So much for Plato.
