The Mathematician's Nightmare

The vision of Professor Squarepunt, after Bertrand Russell’s “The Mathematician’s Nightmare” (1954) · How this was made

Uniforms

  • Squares are tiles
  • Cubes are dice
  • Round numbers are balls
  • Primes are indivisible cylinders
  • Perfect numbers wear crowns
  • Everyone else: a plain figure

Odd numbers are male (straight coats), even numbers female (flared skirts).

Colors

The first seven circles wear the rainbow. 10, 100, 1000 and every power of ten wear white; 13 and 666 wear black.

A number in two categories wears the more honorable uniform: the one fewer numbers share below a million. Perfect 4, cubes 100, squares 1,000, primes 78,498, round 100,000.

Narrated, with a waltz, and every number speaking in its own voice. The voices are AI-generated.
